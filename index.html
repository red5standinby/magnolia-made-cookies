Meet the Baker

Hi, I’m Laura

What started as a way to make my children’s birthdays a little sweeter has grown into something I truly love—creating handcrafted sugar cookies that become part of life’s biggest celebrations.

Every cookie is baked from scratch in my home kitchen, then carefully decorated by hand in small batches. From custom color palettes to the tiniest piped details, every set is thoughtfully designed to reflect your unique occasion.

No shortcuts. No mass production. Just beautiful, delicious cookies made with care, one order at a time.
